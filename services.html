Auto Body Repair Services Offered at Carcis Autobody Repair

Carcis Autobody Repair in Vancouver, Washington offers a full range of auto body repair services, from routine dent repair to fully custom paint and body work. Every service below is available whether the repair is going through an insurance claim or being paid for directly. See how to book a free estimate.

About These Services

Dent Repair

Removal of dings and dents from body panels, restoring a clean, factory-smooth finish with no visible trace of the original damage.

Panel Repair

Repair or replacement of damaged body panels, including doors, fenders, quarter panels, hoods, and bumpers, brought back to factory fit and alignment.

Rust Repair

Rust removal and panel restoration, including work on classic and older vehicles. Read more about this specialty on the About page.

Collision Repair

Repair following a vehicle collision, from minor fender-benders to major accident damage, whether the customer is filing an insurance claim or paying out of pocket.

Paint

Full and partial vehicle paint jobs, color-matched to the vehicle's original factory paint code.

Custom Paint and Design

One-off custom paint jobs and design work built around what the customer wants, rather than a standard factory-match repaint. Examples of this work are shown in the gallery on the home page.

Custom Body Work

Body modification and repair work beyond standard factory-spec repair, for vehicle builds that need something specific done to them.
