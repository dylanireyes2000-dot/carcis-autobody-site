Request a Free Auto Body Repair Estimate in Vancouver, Washington

Carcis Autobody Repair offers free estimates for collision repair, dent and panel repair, rust repair, paint, and custom body work, booked directly online. This page covers what happens during the estimate process and how to prepare for it.

About the Estimate Process

How Booking Works

Estimates are booked through an online calendar. Pick an available time, answer a few short questions about the vehicle and the damage, and the booking is confirmed automatically. See the full list of services Carcis handles.

What Happens During the Estimate

Carlos looks over the vehicle in person at the shop, located at 1520 Washington St, Vancouver, WA 98660, and provides a straight assessment of what's needed and what it costs.

Insurance Claims

If the repair is going through an insurance claim, Carcis works directly with the insurance company on the estimate and repair. Read more about how Carcis handles insurance work on the home page.

Personal Pay Repairs

Customers paying for repairs directly go through the same estimate process as insurance claims, with straightforward pricing and no upsell pressure.

What to Bring

Having the vehicle's year, make, and model on hand speeds up the estimate, along with any details about what happened to cause the damage.

Current Promotion

Carcis is offering 10% off all work through February 14, 2027. Learn more about the shop and its owners on the About page.
